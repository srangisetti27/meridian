Key definitions & assumptions
  • Attainment = Closed-Won ÷ quota. Open pipeline reported separately, never blended.
  • Coverage = open pipeline ÷ remaining quota; 1.28x break-even derived from Q1's realized value win-rate, not an industry heuristic.
  • Same point = equal days elapsed (day 32: May 2 ↔ Feb 1); residual calendar effects favor Q2, so the gap is real.
  • Risk = fixed published thresholds on pace, coverage, and record reliability — not a predictive model. Weighted forecasting deliberately excluded: no stage-outcome history exists to calibrate probabilities honestly.
  • As-of date (May 2, 2026) from the brief — the files carry no snapshot timestamp.

What it proves / doesn't
  • Proves: trustworthy plain-English answers over governed metrics are buildable in take-home scope, with hallucination structurally impossible in the numeric path.
  • Doesn't: production scale, live-CRM messiness, open-ended NL. Next: read-only Salesforce/Snowflake with snapshot journaling — making F4-style re-dating impossible to hide, not just detectable.
